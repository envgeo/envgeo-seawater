# How to cite EnvGeo-Seawater

## Cite the software

Please cite the specific software version used in your work:

> Ishimura, T. (2026). *EnvGeo-Seawater: An Interactive Platform for
> Exploring Seawater Isotope and Hydrographic Data* (Version 1.3.4).
> Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23117784

The DOI above identifies the fixed v1.3.4 release. The all-versions concept
DOI is [10.5281/zenodo.23117783](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23117783).
Use the version DOI whenever the version used in an analysis is known.

## Cite the original data sources

EnvGeo-Seawater brings together cited datasets; it does not replace their
original publications or database records. In addition to citing the software,
please cite the original provider for **every dataset used in an analysis or to
produce a figure or table**.

1. Open **Data Sources** and identify the source dataset or datasets used.
2. Cite the associated original paper, database record, or DOI in your methods,
   figure caption, presentation slide, or data-availability statement.
3. State the selected dataset, filters, and software version clearly enough for
   readers to understand and reproduce the analysis.

The Data Sources tab includes the core Japanese marginal-seas collection and
the external reference datasets, including NASA GISS and PAGES CoralHydro2k.
Only cite datasets that contributed to your result, figure, table, or
presentation; do not cite a comparison dataset that was not used.

For a machine-readable software citation, see
[CITATION.cff](https://github.com/envgeo/seawater_map/blob/main/CITATION.cff).
